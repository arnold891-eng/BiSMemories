BiS> Memories — your album

Point this at your Screenshots folder and it becomes an album you can click through. Everything happens inside your browser: nothing is uploaded, nothing is installed, and it works offline. Add the addon's saved file too and each picture gets its story.

Nothing loaded yet. Click Choose your Screenshots folder above.

It is usually here:

World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\Screenshots
World of Warcraft\_classic_era_\Screenshots

For the captions, the addon's notes file is here — pick your own account and character folder:

World of Warcraft\<version>\WTF\Account\<ACCOUNT>\SavedVariables\BiSMemories.lua

Your browser may ask permission to read the folder. That is the browser protecting you, not this page asking for anything — say yes and the pictures are read from disk and shown. They never leave your machine.
